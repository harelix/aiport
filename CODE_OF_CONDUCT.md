# Code of conduct

aiport follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).
In short: be kind, assume good faith, and keep discussion about the work.

Report unacceptable behaviour privately to the maintainer through the contact on
the [maintainer's GitHub profile](https://github.com/harelix). Reports are kept
confidential, and the maintainer may remove, edit or reject contributions and
block participants who do not follow it.
